## The Human Story Behind Every Ounce of Gold

In the rolling hills of rural Uganda, before the sun paints the sky with orange and gold, miners begin their daily journey. Armed with picks, shovels, and generations of knowledge, they descend into pits carved by hand—some as deep as 30 meters into the earth.

These are not industrial miners with heavy machinery. These are artisanal miners—mothers, fathers, and dreamers—working to build better lives for their families. And their gold tells a story that goes far beyond market prices.

## The Challenge: Exploitation in Traditional Supply Chains

For decades, artisanal miners across East Africa have faced significant challenges:

- **Exploitative Middlemen:** Only receiving 40-60% of market value
- **Unsafe Working Conditions:** No safety equipment or training
- **Lack of Market Access:** No connection to fair international buyers
- **No Documentation:** Unable to prove ethical sourcing
- **Child Labor:** Poverty forcing children into mines

## The Solution: Direct Trade Model

At Huanqiu Precious Metal, we've built our business on a simple principle: **miners deserve fair compensation for their hard work.**

### How Our Direct Trade Model Works

Traditional Model:
Miners → Middlemen (Multiple) → Exporters → International Market
Miners receive: 40-60% of value

Our Direct Trade Model:
Miners → Huanqiu Precious Metal → International Market
Miners receive: 80-90% of value
