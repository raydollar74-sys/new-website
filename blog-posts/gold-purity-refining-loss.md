## The Truth About Gold Refining Loss

One of the most common questions we receive from miners and traders is: **"How much gold do I actually lose when refining from raw purity to investment-grade gold?"**

The answer is crucial for anyone in the precious metals trade. Whether you're a small-scale artisanal miner in Uganda, a trader in the DRC, or an investor in Tanzania, understanding refining losses helps you accurately calculate returns and make informed decisions.

## Understanding Gold Purity Levels

Gold purity is measured in karats or as a percentage. Here's what different purity levels mean:

| Purity Level | Karat | Common Name | Impurities |
|--------------|-------|-------------|------------|
| 99.99% | 24K | Investment Grade | 0.01% |
| 99.5% | 24K | Fine Gold | 0.5% |
| 97.5% | 23K | High Purity | 2.5% |
| 91.67% | 22K | Jewelry Grade | 8.33% |
| 75% | 18K | Standard Jewelry | 25% |

## The Refining Process Explained

When you bring gold for refining, the process doesn't create more gold—it removes impurities. Let's use a practical example:

### Example: Refining 1 Kilogram of 97.5% Gold

**Initial Pure Gold Content:**

1000g × 0.975 = 975g of pure gold

**After Refining to 99.99%:**

Final weight = 975g ÷ 0.9999 ≈ 975.10g

**Total Loss:**

1000g - 975.10g = 24.90g (approximately 2.49%)


## What Are You Actually Losing?

The 2.49% loss is **not gold**—it's the impurities that were originally in the gold:

- **Base Metals:** Copper, silver, zinc
- **Minerals:** Quartz, iron, sulfur compounds
- **Other Elements:** Lead, tin, nickel

These impurities are safely removed through our advanced refining process, leaving you with pure, investment-grade gold that commands premium market prices.

## Factors Affecting Refining Loss

Several factors influence how much material is lost during refining:

### 1. Initial Purity
Lower purity means more impurities to remove. Gold at 75% purity will have higher weight loss than gold at 97.5% purity.

### 2. Impurity Composition
Different impurities require different removal processes. Some are easier to separate than others.

### 3. Refining Method
- **Aqua Regia Process:** Ideal for alloys and scrap
- **Electrolytic Refining:** Produces ultra-pure gold efficiently
- **Fire Assay:** Traditional method for precise measurement

### 4. Laboratory Accuracy
Professional testing ensures you're compensated for the exact gold content, not just estimated values.
